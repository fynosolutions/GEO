VIBE

AYCE hot pot where the seafood is genuinely live-from-the-tank fresh, not frozen-and-thawed fresh. Every table gets its own private room, which makes it feel less like a restaurant and more like you rented out a spot.

WHAT TO GET

Yin-yang broth split (half spicy, half mild), wagyu slices, and whatever's swimming in the tanks up front. Hit the sauce station hard - garlic, chili oil, scallion, soy, raw egg, build your own situation.

THE MOVE

Reservations on weekends, and come genuinely hungry. This is a multiple-hour commitment.

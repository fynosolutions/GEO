The mala broth is a crowd-pleaser, and the buffet spread of veggies, tofu, and noodles makes it genuinely great for vegetarians. Loud, bright, and made for groups - best enjoyed on a cold night with a big table of friends.

VIBE

All-you-can-eat shabu-shabu inside a mall, loud and packed, but the kind of place that earns its crowds on price and volume rather than hype.

WHAT TO GET

Mala broth with a side of mala hot sauce, then soft serve at the end - matcha/vanilla swirl or pineapple Dole Whip (actually vegan).(2)

gonna daydream ab the matcha soft serve FOREVERRRR 🫨 the mala soup base was so yum and i loved how u could add as many veggies as u want! SO VEGETARIAN FRIENDLY — @ashleyc, 1y ago

Obv the hot pot is good but the ice cream was firmly implanted in my childhood memories .. so yum. Also i met that youtuber strictly dumpling here when i was in high school (before i found out about all the cult related things) 😀 — @bladee, 8mo ago

THE MOVE

Weekday lunch (11am-3pm) runs cheaper than dinner, and the wait is more manageable. Whole party needs to be there before they'll seat you.

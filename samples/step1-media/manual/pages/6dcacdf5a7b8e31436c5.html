VIBE

AYCE hotpot and KBBQ under one roof, popular for group hangs and birthday dinners where one person eats free.

WHAT TO GET

The combo option gets you both hotpot and BBQ. Load up at the sauce bar, grab the fried mantou at the station, and stick to tofu skin and fishballs over the harder-to-cook vegetables.

THE MOVE

Reservations help but still expect a wait at peak hours. Come hungry since there's a charge for food waste, and confirm the pricing on the combo before you order.

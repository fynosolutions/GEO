MEILI 沉香

🌶️sichuan & jazz rooftop

160 N 12th St, Brooklyn • Williamsburg

201 SAVES

Sexy Sichuan spot with live jazz, inventive cocktails, and a rooftop bar

From the Michelin-pedigreed chef behind Café China, the food is the real draw - kung pao shrimp, mala mushrooms, and chili oil pork dumplings are standouts. Dine Thursday through Sunday and bring your receipt upstairs for $10 off drinks. Perfect for last-minute dates or group dinners that turn into a full night out.

The green chile breakfast burrito that ruins all other burritos for you

Counter service until 3pm, then sit-down dinner with enchiladas, smothered burritos, and margaritas. The sausage version is the move - crispy hashbrown, soft egg, fresh tortilla, and banging peppers. Go early on weekends or expect a line.

Opera House

225 SAVES

Moody speakeasy beneath a Chinatown restaurant, dripping in history and cinematic charm

Tucked under Chinese Tuxedo on Doyers Street, this intimate bar feels like stepping into an old kung fu film - dark wood, velvet, and a hand-painted mural by the owner's granddaughter.

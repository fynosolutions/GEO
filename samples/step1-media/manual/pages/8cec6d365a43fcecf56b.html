Each diner gets their own pot, with broths ranging from spicy beef tallow to herbal - the Angus beef and live blue crab are highlights. Groups and birthday celebrations fill the place, and the staff actually keeps up. Free dessert soup at the end is a nice touch.

VIBE

AYCE hotpot and BBQ skewers in Chinatown, individual pots at every seat, around $40 a head. The kind of place that works best when you show up hungry with a group.

WHAT TO GET

Beef tongue and Angus slices for the pot, garlic shrimp skewers off the grill, and the off-menu dessert soup they bring at the end.

THE MOVE

Go on a birthday and ask about the discount. Drinks are separate, so factor that in before you commit to the combo.

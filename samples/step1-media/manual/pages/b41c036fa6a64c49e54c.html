VIBE

AYCE hotpot chain with three tiers of pricing, a massive self-serve buffet bar, and a crowd that keeps coming back often enough to have a favorite broth.

WHAT TO GET

The spicy broth (or half-and-half if you're cautious), garlic beef from the meat order, quail eggs from the buffet bar, and build your own dipping sauce at the sauce station. Ask about the ice cream or you'll miss it.

THE MOVE

Make a reservation, especially on weekends. The 90-minute limit is real, so load up on meat orders early and use the buffet bar to fill gaps.

🌶️sichuan hotpot

You pick the chilies, mushrooms, and meats, then watch it come together - either in broth or dry stir-fry style. Modern and bright for a Chinatown spot, which makes the boldness of the food hit even harder. Best with a group willing to get loud and make a mess.

VIBE

DIY hot pot spot where you pick your own ingredients by weight, then tell them soup base or dry pot - staff cooks it for you. More authentic spice levels than you'd expect from a chain.

WHAT TO GET

The dry pot with rice. If you have any heat tolerance questions, order one level down from what you think you want.

THE MOVE

One pound minimum per order, so come hungry or come with people to split across.
